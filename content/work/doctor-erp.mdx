## The problem

In a busy clinic, the file is often found by the receptionist's memory, the new prescription is written without the last one in view, and the evening ends with counting slips to work out the day's collection.

## What we built

One file per patient, found by name, phone or file number. A visit opens with the history in front of the doctor. The prescription, bill and receipt come out of the same visit, and the day's collection is reported per doctor.
