## The problem

A press runs on small pieces of information: size, paper, colour, quantity, deadline, rate, advance. In most shops these live in different places. The job card is a carbon slip. The rate is whatever the owner remembers. The advance is in a notebook next to the till.

When the same customer comes back, nobody is sure what was agreed last time or how much they still owe.

## What we built

A job card for every order, with the rate pulled from the customer's saved prices. Advances and payments post to a ledger under the customer's name. Jobs move from Design to Printing to Ready, and the customer is told on WhatsApp when theirs is done.

## Behind the screens

- Job cards, estimates and a customer ledger
- Advance and payment records by cash, bank, JazzCash and Easypaisa
- Paper and ink stock, machines and staff roles
- Daily collection and outstanding reports, exportable to Excel and PDF
