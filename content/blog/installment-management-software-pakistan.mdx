Selling on installments is a trust business. You hand over goods today and collect the money over months. The sale is the easy part. **The difficult part is keeping track**: who owes what, who is late, who paid last week, and who the guarantor is.

Many shops use registers or Excel sheets for this. They work for a while, then problems start.

## Why registers and loose sheets fail

- **Hard to search.** Finding one customer's history means turning pages.
- **Easy to make mistakes.** One wrong addition and the balance is off for months.
- **No automatic overdue list.** You find out someone is late only when you remember to check.
- **One copy only.** If the register is lost or damaged, the records go with it.
- **Staff dependence.** When a recovery person leaves, their knowledge leaves too.

## The records every installment shop needs

For each sale, keep:

1. **Customer details:** name, CNIC, phone, address, workplace.
2. **Guarantor details:** the same, for each guarantor.
3. **The item sold:** model, serial number, price.
4. **The plan:** total price, advance, number of installments, amount and due dates.
5. **Every payment:** date, amount, who received it, and the receipt.
6. **The current balance** and next due date.
7. **Notes:** promises, extensions, visits.

## What installment software should do

### Plans in a few clicks

Enter the price, advance and number of installments, and the software generates the schedule. No manual calendar work.

### A clear customer and guarantor record

One page per customer with the plan, payments, balance and guarantor. Everything is in one search.

### A daily recovery list

Every morning, see who is due today and who is overdue, with the amount. Assign people to follow up and note the result.

### WhatsApp reminders

A polite reminder before the due date, and a follow-up after, reduces late payments without awkward conversations.

### Credit history

If a customer has bought from you before, see how reliably they paid. It helps you decide about the next sale.

### Reports

Daily collection, total outstanding and overdue by customer, so you know the real position of your business.

## Questions to ask before choosing software

- Can I search for a customer by name, phone or CNIC?
- Does it generate a recovery list for each day?
- Does it support WhatsApp reminders?
- Can I print receipts and statements?
- Is my data backed up, and can I export it?
- Is it designed for how **Pakistani shops** work?
