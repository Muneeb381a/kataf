## The problem

Selling on installments means trusting people over many months. The records for that trust are usually a register, a few slips and the recovery staff's memory. A guarantor's number is on a different page. A customer who paid late three times looks the same as one who never missed.

## What we built

A plan for each sale, with the full schedule of due dates generated from price, advance and months. The customer's guarantor is linked to the plan. Reminders go out on WhatsApp before the due date, and the recovery list shows what is due today and what is overdue.
