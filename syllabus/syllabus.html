03 / How we teach

We do not just teach. You get full hands-on experience.

01
You work in every session

Each class is a working session. You do the task yourself, on your own computer, with the instructor beside you.

02
Every module ends in a build

No module is finished until you have made something: a design, a page, an API, a video, a spreadsheet.

03
Feedback on your own work

We review what you made, show you what to fix, and you fix it.

04
A capstone you can show

Every course ends with a project for your portfolio.

04 / Assessment, certificate and internship

How you are assessed, and what you receive.

Assessment
  • A hands-on lab in every session, reviewed by the instructor.
  • A deliverable at the end of every module.
  • A capstone project reviewed against a clear checklist.
Certificate
  • Awarded when every module deliverable and the capstone are complete.
  • Carries your name, the course and a unique ID.
  • Anyone can verify it online at kataf.pk/verify.
Internship

An internship follows every course, working with the team on real tasks with feedback. Estimated length: 4 to 8 weeks, part-time. Dates, hours and terms are explained at admission, before you pay.

Batches

Men and women study in separate batches with their own timings. Choose your batch and a preferred slot on the admission form.
